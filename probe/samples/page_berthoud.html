Employment Opportunities

Thank you for your interest in employment with the Town of Berthoud!

A Town of Berthoud employment application must be completed for each position you are applying for.

Incomplete applications will not be accepted. A resume will not substitute for a completed application. 

The Town of Berthoud is an Equal Opportunity Employer. Qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability or protected veteran status.


  1. How to Apply
  2. Employee Benefit Information
  1. How to Apply

Please visit the Employment Opportunities page to view available employment opportunities.  Internal employment opportunities available to Town of Berthoud employees may be viewed by visiting the Internal Employment Opportunities page.

Applications are accepted using the online employment application form.  Applicants are encouraged to attach a resume and cover letter.  A resume cannot be used to substitute a complete employment application.  



  1. Employee Benefit Information

Employee Benefit Overview

The Town of Berthoud offers generous benefits for eligible employees.  These benefits are subject to change with or without notice.

Group Health Insurance

All permanent full-time employees are eligible for a high-deductible HSA health plan.  The Town will contribute to the employee's deductible by fully funding the employee's health savings account.  

Coverage is paid in full by employer for employee only coverage.  Spouse, child, and family coverage are subject to eligibility requirements in the employee handbook.  

CoverageEmployee Contribution
Employee OnlyPaid by Employer
Employee + Spouse $100.00 / Month
Employee + Child$100.00 / Month
Family Coverage$150.00 / Month


Vision insurance 

The Town provides vision insurance to eligible employees through Guardian.  The Guardian vision plan offers coverage using the VSP Choice Network. 

Dental Coverage

Dental insurance is provided through Guardian at two different plan levels for eligible employees.  

Additional Benefits

The Town offers a generous package of additional benefits:

  • Virtual care through Healthiestyou by Teledoc
  • Basic and voluntary life insurance 
  • Employee Assistance Program
  • Accident Coverage
  • Longevity Pay 
  • Berthoud Recreation Center membership
  • Up to 12 paid holidays 
  • Vacation leave begins accruing immediately 

These benefits are subject to change with or without notice.  Not all employees are eligible to receive all benefits as described above.  Not all benefits described above are paid for by the Town. 

Machine Readable Files

Machine-readable files are made available in response to the federal Transparency in Coverage Rule and includes negotiated service rates and out-of-network allowed amounts between health plans and healthcare providers. The machine readable files are formatted to allow researchers, regulators, and application developers to more easily access and analyze data.
